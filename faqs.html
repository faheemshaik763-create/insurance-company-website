Frequently Asked Questions

Find clear answers regarding policy activation, 24-hour claim payouts, tax benefits, and cashless hospital rules.

How fast are claims settled at Faheem Insurance Company?
Under Executive Officer Faheem's priority response system, verified cashless hospitalizations and auto repair claims are processed and approved within 4 hours. Bank transfer reimbursements are disbursed within 24 hours.
What tax benefits can I claim with health and life insurance policies?
Health insurance premiums qualify for income tax deductions under Section 80D (or equivalent local regulations). Life insurance death benefits are 100% tax-free under Section 10(10D).
How does cashless hospitalization work at network hospitals?
Simply present your Digital Insurance ID card (available in your Client Portal) at any partner hospital desk. The hospital contacts Faheem Insurance directly for instant pre-authorization.
Can I schedule a 1-on-1 consultation directly with Officer Faheem's advisory team?
Yes! You can book an appointment on our Contact page or call our toll-free executive desk at +1 (800) 555-FAHEEM for personalized portfolio auditing.
